Concept

the first part is same as Steepest Descent Method, but after find the new , we calculate next instead of finding steepest descent by

and

x0 = initial guess
g0 = \nabla f(x0)
s0 = g0
for k = 0, 1, 2,...
	Choose αk to minimize f(xk + αk sk)
	xk+1 = xk + αk sk
	gk+1 = \nabla f(xk+1)
	βk+1 = (g^Tk+1 gk+1)/(g^Tk gk)
	sk+1 = gk+1 + βk+1sk
end
